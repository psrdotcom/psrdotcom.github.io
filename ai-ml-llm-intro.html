What are Large Language Models?

LLMs are a type of GenAI trained on vast amounts of text data from the internet. Their ability to process language allows them to:

  • Answer complex questions
  • Summarize long documents
  • Translate between languages
  • Write source code

Agentic AI: Your Personal Assistant

Agentic AI takes instructions and formulates a multi-step plan to achieve a goal. It acts as an autonomous agent.

Example Task:

"Plan and book a weekend trip." The AI would find flights, compare hotels, and complete the bookings on your behalf.

RAG: Smarter with External Data

Problem: LLMs can be outdated. Solution: Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) connects them to live knowledge bases.
